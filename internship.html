Internship Experiences

Acquiring valuable industry experience by working on innovative projects and learning from experienced professionals.

Indian Railways

Observed and studied communication systems used in railway operations, including station-to-station and train-to-station communication networks. Gained exposure to wireless communication bands, frequency allocation, signaling systems, safety protocols, and railway operational infrastructure.

PWD - Jodhpur

Observed and studied electrical installations and infrastructure in government buildings under the Public Works Department (PWD). Gained practical exposure to building wiring systems, electrical connections for elevators, power distribution networks, and backup power arrangements. Learned about hospital electrical systems, including emergency power supply and automatic operation of critical equipment during power outages. Assisted in understanding electrical safety practices, maintenance procedures, and building services engineering.

DRDO - Jodhpur

During my internship at the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO), Jodhpur, I explored key concepts of instrumentation and electronics, including signal conditioning, analog-to-digital conversion, sensor integration, and PCB design fundamentals. This experience enhanced my understanding of real-world electronic systems, data acquisition techniques, and embedded hardware development.
